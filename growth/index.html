Questions we get a lot

How dark can I go in Arizona?

Front side windows must let in more than 33% of light, so 35% is the darkest legal choice. Rear side and back windows can be any shade. Clear film is legal on the full windshield.

Can I roll my windows down after tint?

Wait three days in summer, five in winter. The film needs time to cure against the glass.

Does ceramic tint block my phone or GPS?

No. Ceramic film has no metal in it, so phone, GPS and toll transponders work normally.

What does the warranty cover?

Tint is covered for life against bubbling, peeling and fading. PPF carries a 10-year manufacturer warranty against yellowing and cracking.

Do I need to leave a deposit?

No deposit for tint. Film and coating jobs over $1,000 need a $100 deposit, applied to your invoice.
